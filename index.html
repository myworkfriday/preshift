SERVICE — THE FOUR ACTIONS (EVERY SHIFT)

Service is the actions — what you do. Hospitality is the feeling — how the guest feels while you do it, and that's different for every guest. Teach the actions; the feeling is the read.

  • Greet & Guide — 60 seconds. Name, welcome, "when was the last time you were here?", and a beverage recommendation that starts the meal right. You take control of the table's start.
  • Order & Confirm — guide, don't just write: "the short rib is the move tonight." Confirm temps, sides, mods, allergies. Repeat it back.
  • Deliver & Maintain — food to the right seat, never auction. Refills, clear empties, "your Brussels are still coming." Keep the table moving.
  • Check & Close — back in 2 minutes or 2 bites. Dessert before the check. Close it warm — you end the meal, you don't just drop a bill.

Today’s focus: _______________________

Why Upselling beverage

The beverage recommendation is a service, not a sales pitch — guests genuinely want help deciding. One specific, confident recommendation beats a menu left open. And the check average follows the service.

The how, in one line each:

  • Every table gets a beverage recommendation. Every time. No exceptions.
  • Formula: Ask what they normally enjoy, Listen, Recommend one or two tailored options, Personalize — tell them WHY.
  • Bar: offer 2 specific LBW or mocktail recommendations. Upsell water — "still, tap or sparkling?"
  • Deliver a specific refill, never "another drink?" — "Can I get you another Coke? Another Tito's and soda?"
