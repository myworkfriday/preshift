SERVICE — THE FOUR ACTIONS (EVERY SHIFT)

Service is the actions — what you do. Hospitality is the feeling — how the guest feels while you do it, and that's different for every guest. Teach the actions; the feeling is the read.

  • Greet & Guide — 60 seconds. Name, welcome, "been here before?", and a beverage recommendation that starts the meal right. You take control of the table's start.
  • Order & Confirm — guide, don't just write: "the short rib is the move tonight." Confirm temps, sides, mods, allergies. Repeat it back.
  • Deliver & Maintain — food to the right seat, never auction. Refills, clear empties, "your Brussels are still coming." Keep the table moving.
  • Check & Close — back in 2 minutes or 2 bites. Dessert before the check. Close it warm — you end the meal, you don't just drop a bill.

Why Bar communication

The bar and the floor run one service, not two. Communication means the ticket is spoken, not assumed: tell the bar when a party lands, call the 86s out loud, say it when a drink is taking too long. "If something goes wrong — communicate. If an item is 86'd — communicate. Silence costs you guests."

The how, in one line each:

  • Call your tickets. Say the order back, name the guest count, flag the mods.
  • Consolidate — every trip does two jobs: deliver food AND refill drinks, drop condiments AND clear plates.
  • Stay visible. Never let a guest wonder where their server went. Circulation is communication too.
  • Watch the well. Beverages are a 2-5 minute standard. If a drink sits, say so before the guest asks.
